## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

- ❌ **Script Does Not Execute After Pasting**
  The runner might be outdated or the game client patched since your last session.
  **Solution:** Update your runner to the latest version and re-download the Murder Mystery 2 Script from the official source.

- ❌ **Murder Mystery 2 Script ESP Not Showing Players**
  This usually happens when the ESP module loads before the round fully initializes.
  **Solution:** Wait until you are fully loaded into a round, then toggle the Murder Mystery 2 Script ESP off and back on using `F1`.

- ❌ **Freeze Trade Does Not Lock the Window**
  The Murder Mystery 2 Script Freeze Trade feature requires both players to have the trade window open.
  **Solution:** Only activate Freeze Trade after the other player has confirmed the initial trade request.

- ❌ **Hitbox Expander Causes Visible Lag**
  Running the Murder Mystery 2 Script Hitbox at high multiplier values on low-end hardware can tank your framerate.
  **Solution:** Lower the `hitbox_size` value in your `config.lua` to `3` or `4` and close background applications.

- ❌ **Admin Panel Does Not Open With F9**
  A keybind conflict with another application or the game itself is blocking the input.
  **Solution:** Change the `admin_panel_key` value in your configuration file to an unused key like `F10` or `F12`.

- ❌ **Auto Shoot Fires at Innocents**
  The targeting logic may misidentify roles if the round data has not fully synced.
  **Solution:** Enable `RoleAlerts` in your config so the Murder Mystery 2 Script Auto Shoot only activates after role assignment is confirmed.
